A garden clean up in Frankston usually has a deadline attached

Almost every garden clean up we do in Frankston is driven by a date. Either there is a final inspection and a bond on the line, or the property is going to market and the photographer is booked for Thursday. Both mean the yard has to go from wherever it is now to presentable, in one visit, by a fixed day.

Tell us the date when you call. We work backwards from it, and if it is not achievable we will say so straight away rather than taking the booking and letting you find out on the day.

End-of-lease clean-ups

Property managers assess a yard on a short list of things: is the lawn cut and edged, are the beds free of weeds, are the hedges in shape, are the paths and paving clear, and is there any rubbish left on the property. We do all of it, including hauling away whatever has accumulated down the side of the house over the tenancy.

The maths is simple. A clean-up costs a fraction of what a property manager will charge back against your bond to send their own contractor, and you get to be there and see it done.

Pre-sale garden makeovers

Buyers look at a photo for about two seconds. An unedged lawn and a weedy front bed drag down the first impression of a house that is otherwise fine. A pre-sale tidy-up — lawn cut and edged, mulch topped up, hedges shaped, paths pressure-swept and clear — is one of the cheapest things you can do before listing, and it shows up in the photos immediately.

We work with agents and vendors across Frankston, Frankston South, Seaford and the Peninsula, and we can schedule around the photography date so the garden peaks on the right day.

What a clean-up covers

Lawns cut, often in two passes if it is deep, then caught and edged. Beds weeded by hand and re-mulched where needed. Hedges and shrubs cut back into shape. Paths, paving and drives cleared and blown. Then every bit of it — clippings, prunings, weeds and any rubbish left on site — loaded and taken away. You are not left with the mess or a full green bin.
